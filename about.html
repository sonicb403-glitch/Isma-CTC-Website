About Our Club

Club Background

Isma CTC is a student club created to provide students with opportunities to learn and explore different areas of computer technology.

The club encourages students to share knowledge, develop technical skills and work together on technology-related projects.

Our Vision

To become a supportive community that inspires students to develop their technology skills, creativity and innovation.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide students with useful activities, workshops and learning opportunities related to computers and technology.
